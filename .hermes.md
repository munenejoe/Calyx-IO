## Purpose

CALYX FLORA is a botanical intelligence platform centered around accurate flower and plant identification.

The long-term vision is to build a modular machine learning ecosystem that identifies species through trait extraction rather than relying on a single monolithic classifier. The identification engine will eventually support a community platform where plant enthusiasts can discover, document, discuss, and share botanical observations.

The project is designed to remain lightweight enough for cloud deployment while being extensible enough to evolve into a complete botanical platform.
